Imagine adding friendly column headers to a CSV export. An analyst gets a readable report; an automated consumer expecting stable headers breaks. "It's only a checkbox" has become a compatibility promise.

AI makes the code draft cheaper. It does not make compatibility, saved preferences, support, or future migrations disappear. Five independent binary settings imply up to 32 configurations, though not necessarily 32 required tests.

Before adding the switch, identify who consumes the export and which headers they depend on. Compare independent settings with named Data export and Report export contracts. Named contracts may be too restrictive for users who need independent control; either design owes existing consumers a migration plan.

Where would you draw the boundary between a flexible export and a stable contract? I'd like to hear about a consumer dependency or user need that made the tidy design the wrong choice.
