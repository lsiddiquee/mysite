A €20 pump got our Roborock dock working again.

It had stopped dispensing water to wash the mops. A replacement dock would have cost around €300 or more.

The casing cost me ten minutes of cautious pulling. Online information didn't show me how it locked, so I opened the bottom panel to see the clips. They needed to be pushed inward to unlock. Once I knew that, the rest was easy.

I've marked the tabs and locks in my photo so the next person can find them without opening the bottom just to discover the mechanism.

The pump moved water when turned by hand, but an upstream power or control fault was still possible. Without a stable 12 V supply to test the motor separately, I tried the inexpensive replacement first.

Would you try a €20 replacement first, or trace the electrical fault before ordering parts?
