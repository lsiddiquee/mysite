Disclaimer: a break from software posts. This time, I brought AI a phone photo instead of code.

Our Roborock dock said the mop wash was complete, but the clean tank was full and the dirty tank empty. Once inside, I photographed what I could trace and asked why I could see a motor but no pump.

AI explained the peristaltic head. Turning it by hand moved water; a syringe test showed an open downstream path. Those observations gave us better questions, not a certain electrical diagnosis. A €20 replacement restored washing, against roughly €300 for another dock.

Finding the casing clips was my own exploration. Unplug and drain first; inspect attached wiring, avoid circuitry and live testing, and use a repair service if access isn't safe.
