Gitea worked on my Synology NAS. CI couldn't even start checkout.

The runner's Docker-in-Docker daemon failed while mounting OverlayFS for a job container. Adding privileged mode wasn't the answer: it was already enabled.

I replaced the nested daemon with Synology's existing Docker daemon, mounted its socket, and preserved the runner registration directory. Then I had to create the missing runner config and use the actual Compose network name: gitea_gitea, not gitea.

A job container started and CI began progressing. The startup failure was resolved; the rest of the pipeline still needed its own checks.

The cost matters: Docker socket access gives CI extensive control over the NAS. Removing privileged mode doesn't make that safe for untrusted workflows.
