STORY:
Engineering planning still schedules around a constraint that has moved. Story points priced an
effort spread that collapsed to zero. Dependency ordering was insurance against rework that got
cheap. The frozen API contract was a scheduling instrument, not a correctness one. And the argument
does not rest on "AI made typing fast": it rests on three different rates on one path, where
generation went up by orders of magnitude, review went up and then hit a ceiling, and organizational
sign-off did not move at all. The slowest of the three now governs arrival, and the whole measuring
apparatus is still pointed at the fastest.

One single heavy rail runs the full width of the frame, so there is exactly one path and no
branching diagram. The same piece of hardware appears twice on that rail, in opposite states, and
that repetition carries the entire thesis in one glance: a pair of blocks facing each other across
the rail, thrown fully wide apart at the old station on the left-centre, and an identical pair
squeezed almost shut at the new station on the right. The bottleneck did not disappear. It relocated
downstream, and the instrument did not follow.

The apparatus is described as physically incapable of having turned, because that is the claim. It
is bolted to a base plate, braced by diagonal struts, and its caliper jaws are closed tip to tip
around the empty rail at the old station, clamped on nothing. Jaws shut to zero width is the
story-point beat stated as geometry: the spread it was built to measure went to zero and it is still
measuring. A flat hard-edged wedge of attention drops from its head onto that same empty gap, and it
is drawn deliberately oversized. At card size that wedge is the element carrying "attention aimed at
nothing", and the first render made it small enough to vanish, which left the picture with no visible
claim at all. Its size is therefore specified three independent ways: as a fraction of the frame,
about one fifth of the width at its base; as a multiple of a work unit, roughly five squares wide;
and as a rank, the largest and palest single area of flat colour in the picture. The negative prompt
rejects the thin-beam reading.

The misdirection is stated redundantly so a diffusion model cannot quietly correct it into a
sensible picture of an instrument inspecting a jam. Five cues all point the same way. First, the
jaws physically enclose bare rail with no work unit between them. Second, the wedge of attention
lands entirely on bare rail and touches no unit anywhere. Third, an obvious horizontal band of empty
background separates the right edge of the wedge from the first stalled unit, so the two never
overlap. Fourth, the rig is bolted and braced, with its head turned away from the right side of the
frame. Fifth, the prompt says outright that nothing in the picture points at, reaches toward,
inspects, illuminates, or is connected to the pinch, and the negative prompt repeats it. The
apparatus must never be shown aimed right, rotating, tilting toward the jam, or linked to it by any
line, beam, arm, cable, or dashed path.

Which side of the pinch the queue stands on is the second thing a model will quietly correct, and the
first render corrected it: the stopped units came back to the RIGHT of the black blocks, which reads
as work that got through and then parked, the opposite of a bottleneck. Flow runs left to right, so a
blockage backs up on its LEFT. That claim gets the same four-way treatment. Geometry: the queue
occupies the stretch of rail between the centre of the frame and the pinch, never past it. Adjacency:
the leading square's right edge touches the left face of the black blocks, and the other three back up
behind it toward the left. Explicit negative: the rail from the pinch to the right edge of the frame
is bare, with no square, fragment, or streak on it, because nothing has got through. Physical simile:
traffic stopped behind a closed barrier, where the line builds on the approach side and the road
beyond stays empty. The negative prompt repeats it.

The three rates are encoded as three readable properties of the same object along one line, so the
lane idea survives without a second competing subject or a chart. Spacing goes wide, then medium,
then touching. Motion streaks go long, then short, then none. Angle goes tilted, then slightly
tilted, then dead square. The units themselves never change colour or shape, so a viewer reads a
change in rate rather than a change in kind.

Stalled must not curdle into overloaded, so the queue is deliberately small and tidy. It is exactly
four or five units in a single file, square-on, evenly aligned, touching, sitting flat on the rail,
not cropped by the frame edge, with nothing stacked above them and nothing scattered around them.
There is no heap, no mass, no tumbling, no cropped-unbounded backlog, and no pressure or strain
marks. The units are simply wider than the slit and they have stopped. Calm and finite is the point:
this is a stall, not a flood.

Every trace of remedy stays out of the frame. Nothing widens the pinch, no second instrument arrives,
no arrow reroutes the flow, there is no checkmark, no after-state, and no second panel. The picture
is one uncorrected mistake held still.

At thumbnail size the frame carries three tonal roles. A heavy deep-plum apparatus anchored in the
